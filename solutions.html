Retail & Malls

Maximize visual footfall and brand engagement. Our double-sided storefront screens and interactive mapping totems guide shoppers while displaying bright, dynamic campaigns that fight natural window glare.

  • Interactive wayfinding directories and kiosks
  • Dynamic video walls for product launch backdrops

Hospitality & Hotels

Create a luxurious lobby first impression. Integrate fine-pitch architectural LED columns, digital check-in counters, and conference digital banners to coordinate guest flow seamlessly.

  • Curved flexible LED column wraps for custom architecture
  • Digital signage door plates showing real-time event status
  • Check-in front counter dynamic backdrop LEDs

Healthcare & Clinics

Reduce waiting anxiety and improve patient navigation. Interactive touch directories assist clinic navigation, while waiting area displays sync live queuing data with health awareness video feeds.

  • Patient queue display panels synced with clinic booking software
  • Anti-shatter interactive directories for sanitization zones
  • Digital notice boards showing emergency room statuses

Education & Campus

Deliver immersive teaching and coordinate campus information. Interactive digital lecture boards, outdoor campus notice pillars, and emergency alerts integrate under a unified network control matrix.

  • Emergency network notification system overrides

Corporate Offices

Empower team collaboration and corporate identity. High-resolution boardroom video wall screens combine with check-in kiosks and smart room booking tablets to create a modern workspace.

  • Internal KPI dashboard grids for lobby reception areas
