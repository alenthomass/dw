03. Site Preconditions & Power

Successful display installation requires rigorous architectural and electrical preparation. Prior to mobilization of our engineering crew, the Client is responsible for ensuring:

  • Clean Power Distribution: Dedicated, surge-protected electrical distribution boards with stable voltage (±5%), neutral-to-ground isolation (< 2V), and independent circuit breakers calibrated to the peak in-rush current of the specified LED/LCD system.
  • Structural Weight Load Bearing: Certified structural backing (steel uni-strut frames, concrete reinforcement, or load-rated drywall headers) verified by the Client’s base-build structural consultant to support the total static and dynamic load of the display.
  • Thermal Ventilation & HVAC: Adequate ambient cooling maintaining operating environments within -10°C to +40°C for indoor displays, with required CFM airflow clearances behind recessed video walls.
  • Network & Data Drops: Gigabit Ethernet termination at the media player location, isolated VLAN routing, and outward internet gateway bandwidth for cloud CMS synchronization.

04. Delivery & Installation

Display World manages comprehensive delivery, unboxing, precision mounting, and calibration under stringent safety standards:

  • Access Permits: The Client shall arrange all necessary building management access permits, loading bay reservations, hot-work permits, and security clearance for our certified installation engineers.
  • Storage & Staging: If the installation site is not ready on the confirmed delivery date, dry, secure, dust-free warehouse storage must be provided. Additional demurrage or storage charges may apply if site delays exceed fifteen (15) days.
  • Health & Safety: Our personnel operate in strict adherence to Dubai Municipality Health and Safety codes, utilizing scaffolding, scissor lifts, and full PPE during all elevated rigging operations.

05. Commissioning & Handover

Following mechanical assembly and cabling, Display World conducts our proprietary 5-Stage Commissioning Protocol:

  1. Module-level color uniformity and gray-scale calibration utilizing scientific photometers.
  2. Cabinet seam micro-adjustment to eliminate panel gaps (< 0.1mm tolerance).
  3. 72-hour continuous thermal and full-white burn-in test to expose infant mortality defects.
  4. CMS video processor resolution scaling, latency verification, and failover switch testing.
  5. Formal client demonstration and issuance of the Certificate of Completion & Handover.

Final acceptance shall be deemed confirmed upon Client signature of the Handover Certificate or upon Client placing the display into public or commercial broadcast use.

06. Warranty & Pixel Policy

All brand-new LED cabinets, video wall displays, and interactive touch kiosks supplied by Display World are backed by our comprehensive warranty:

  • Standard Warranty Duration: Twenty-four (24) to thirty-six (36) months from handover date covering manufacturing defects, power supply units, sending/receiving cards, and panel drivers.
  • Pixel Defect Policy: Fine-pitch LED panels adhere to ISO 13406-2 Class II standards. Any cluster of defective pixels (> 0.003% of total display resolution) will prompt complimentary on-site module replacement using factory spare batches.
  • Exclusions: Warranty does not cover damages resulting from external voltage surges, water ingress into non-waterproof models, physical impact/vandalism, unauthorized third-party repairs, or failure to maintain required ambient HVAC temperatures.
