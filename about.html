About Display World

We're a Dubai team of display engineers and installers. Our founders spent years designing screens for manufacturers before starting Display World.

How we started

Screens in malls and airports run all day in serious heat, so a consumer TV on a bracket doesn't last. Every system we install is calibrated, colour matched and mounted for continuous use.

Our office is in Dubai Investment Park, and our engineers do on-site support across the UAE.

20+ Years in display engineering
500+ Systems installed
15+ Industries served

How we work

Three things we check on every job.

Accurate colour

We calibrate screens to the D65 standard so colours match your brand, with a Delta E below 0.5.

Built for the heat

Die-cast aluminium frames and proper ventilation keep screens running through UAE summers.

No black screens

Critical screens get a backup signal path and dual power supplies, so one fault doesn't take them down.
