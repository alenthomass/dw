FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to typical design layout and consulting queries.

What is your typical turnaround?

Typically, structural design drawings take 3-5 business days, while hardware integration and on-site sync take 10-15 days depending on scale.

Do you assist with structural engineering?

Yes. Our in-house structural engineers map secure mount brackets, weight tolerances, and thermal dissipation calculations.

What calibration standard do you use?

We calibrate to the D65 video standard using high-precision color spectrophotometers to maintain delta matching ratings under 0.5.
