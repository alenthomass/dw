Privacy
Policy

Display World is committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting corporate and personal data collected through our digital platforms, project inquiries, and signage service operations.

01. Data Controller & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Display World Digital Signage LLC (“Display World”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), registered in the Emirate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, collects, stores, processes, and protects information gathered through:

  • Our primary web domain (displayworldme.com and hosted mirrors).
  • Inquiry, quotation, and RFP consultation forms for commercial LED, LCD, and interactive kiosk installations.
  • On-site site survey records, calibration tickets, and Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) support logs.
  • Direct corporate communications via email, telephone, WhatsApp, and in-person consultations.

By using our website, submitting an inquiry, or engaging Display World for audiovisual integration services, you acknowledge the collection and processing of your data as outlined in this policy.

02. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide tailored digital signage engineering, quotation, procurement, and ongoing AMC support:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Details: Full name, official corporate email address, contact telephone number, job title, and organization name.
  • Project Specifications: Architectural drawings, display dimensions, indoor/outdoor environmental requirements, pixel pitch preferences, and site installation coordinates.
  • Service Requests: AMC tier preferences (Bronze, Silver, Gold), breakdown descriptions, and hardware serial numbers submitted via our Help Center.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you browse our digital platforms, our servers automatically log standard network telemetry including your IP address, browser type, operating system version, referring URLs, access timestamps, and device display metrics.

03. Telemetry & Hardware Logs

Note for Enterprise Signage Deployments: Display World strictly respects corporate privacy. Hardware telemetry gathered through CMS monitoring agents is utilized solely for uptime diagnostics, thermal management, and preventive maintenance.

For clients enrolled in our Remote Monitoring and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), our diagnostic systems collect operational data from deployed signage controllers, including:

  • Controller and sending card operating temperatures, voltage draws, and receiving card link states.
  • Panel pixel failure matrices (dead pixel clusters) for automated warranty dispatch.
  • Media player network ping latency, storage buffer thresholds, and firmware version levels.

We do not capture, record, or retain any public audience video feeds or end-user surveillance media from our digital screens or interactive touch kiosks.

04. How We Use Your Data

Display World utilizes collected information exclusively for legitimate business and contractual purposes:

  • Engineering & Quotations: Generating accurate technical proposals, photometric calculations, structural rigging assessments, and commercial bids.
  • Project Delivery: Coordinating equipment procurement, customs clearance, freight logistics, and site installation teams across the GCC.
  • Service Level Commitments: Fulfilling AMC emergency dispatch, remote diagnostics, and scheduled preventive maintenance visits within guaranteed response windows.
  • Customer Communication: Answering inquiries, providing firmware updates, and dispatching warranty certificates.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Complying with UAE accounting, taxation (FTA VAT), and commercial licensing mandates.

05. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your corporate confidentiality. Display World does not sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party advertisers or marketers. Information is disclosed only under the following strictly controlled scenarios:

  • Authorized Hardware Manufacturers: Sharing project site specs and device serial numbers with verified Tier-1 manufacturing partners (e.g., Novastar, Samsung, LG, Absen) strictly for equipment warranty registration and RMA chip replacement.
  • Certified Logistics & Rigging Contractors: Providing site address and delivery coordinator contacts to bonded freight forwarders and certified structural crane operators for safe delivery.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Hosting inquiries and diagnostic records on certified, enterprise-grade cloud environments (Google Cloud / Firebase) adhering to ISO/IEC 27001 data protection standards.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Where compelled by law, court order, or official mandate issued by UAE judicial authorities.

06. Security & Storage Standards

We implement comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All web sessions are secured using TLS 1.3 encryption with 256-bit cryptographic ciphers.
  • Access Control: Administrative and support databases are restricted via role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and audited session credentials.
  • Data Retention: Inquiry data is maintained for the duration of the commercial relationship plus standard statutory warranty periods (typically 3 to 5 years under UAE commercial code).

07. UAE Law & International Standards

Display World operates in full alignment with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL), as well as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for international partners and multinational corporate clients.

Data transferred across borders for multinational project coordination is safeguarded by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and stringent data processing agreements requiring equivalent levels of security.

08. Your Data Subject Rights

Subject to applicable UAE federal regulations and contractual requirements, you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a full copy of the personal information Display World holds regarding you or your organization.
  • Right to Rectification: Request prompt correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal contact records when no longer necessary for ongoing warranty, active AMC contracts, or legal obligations.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to processing of contact details for commercial newsletters or technical case study announcements.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Governance team at info@displayworldme.com.

09. Cookies & Tracking

Our website utilizes strictly essential session cookies and local storage tokens (such as theme preferences and simulated 3D room presets) to ensure rapid loading, responsive rendering, and smooth navigation. We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.
